Hello, Gilmerton!

Gilmerton Nursery

From three months to five years, your child can enjoy well-equipped playrooms, cosy story corners, creative and sensory activities, garden time and regular walks. Pre-school learning incorporates Scotland’s Curriculum for Excellence, while families of younger children receive daily feedback sheets.

Hello, Bonnyrigg!

Bonnyrigg Nursery

Your baby can enjoy a gentle home-from-home introduction with plenty of cuddles and tactile play. As your child grows, purpose-built resources, imaginative activities and Curriculum for Excellence experiences can help their confidence, language and independence flourish.

Our aim

Big wonder begins with a bright start

At Start Bright Nursery, we aim to provide a safe, happy and stimulating environment where every child feels valued. We support children to learn through play, develop confidence and build strong foundations for their future.

  • Kind, caring and welcoming environments
  • Learning shaped around every individual child
  • Strong partnerships with parents and carers
  • Plenty of room for imagination, curiosity and fun
